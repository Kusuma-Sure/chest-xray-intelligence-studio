04 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

About this capstone

Four architecture families are trained and evaluated through a shared CheXpert pipeline.

01

ViT-B/16

Vision Transformer for patch-based global image representation. Gradient × activation attribution is exposed from the final transformer block.

02

DenseNet121

Densely connected convolutional network with a 13-label classification head and Grad-CAM attribution.

03

CapsNet

Convolutional encoder, primary capsules and dynamic routing. Disease capsule lengths are converted to logits for multi-label training.

04

Graph Attention Network

CNN feature cells are represented as a 7×7 grid graph and processed with multi-head graph attention.

Label policy: this baseline maps uncertain labels (-1) and missing labels (NaN) to 0, matching the supplied training notes. This is an experimental assumption, not a statement that uncertain or unmentioned findings are clinically negative.
Research prototype only. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis, triage or treatment decisions.
