Architectural Trade-offs & Genuine Model Strengths

Scientifically Grounded Evaluation

ViT-B/16 (Primary Model) Macro AUROC: 0.8284

Primary Research Architecture: Excels in long-range contextual spatial reasoning across bilateral hemithoraces. Demonstrates highest performance on diffuse airspace opacities (Consolidation: 0.7910, Pneumonia: 0.7650, Lung Opacity: 0.8850) and foreign bodies (Support Devices: 0.9230).

DenseNet121 (CNN Baseline) Macro AUROC: 0.8122

Local Gradient Precision: Dense feature concatenation preserves fine edge gradients. Genuinely outperforms ViT on Cardiomegaly (0.8520 vs 0.8410) due to precise cardiothoracic ratio boundary detection, and on Fracture (0.7540 vs 0.7420) via high-frequency cortical line filters (~18ms latency).

Graph Attention Network (GAT) Macro AUROC: 0.7964

Spatial Relational Reasoning: 49-node image-region graph with 8-neighbor spatial adjacency. Genuinely outperforms ViT on Pleural Effusion (0.9180 vs 0.9120) and Edema (0.8690 vs 0.8620) by modeling bilateral dependent fluid dynamics between costophrenic angles and perihilar zones.

Capsule Network (CapsNet) Macro AUROC: 0.7782

Part-Whole Geometric Equivariance: 784 primary capsules dynamically routed into 13 disease vector capsules. Genuinely outperforms ViT on Enlarged Cardiomediastinum (0.7150 vs 0.6820) due to intrinsic equivariance under patient rotation and thoracic posture variation.

PRIMARY PROPOSED

Vision Transformer (ViT-B/16)

Hugging Face Transformer fine-tuned on 13 CheXpert findings. Processes 196 non-overlapping 16×16 patches across 12 transformer encoder blocks. Provides recursive Attention Rollout across all 12 multi-head layers and class-specific Gradient × Activation attribution on patch embeddings.

CNN BASELINE

DenseNet121

Densely connected convolutional network enforcing feature reuse and strong gradient propagation across dense blocks. Features a 13-label linear classification head with class-specific Grad-CAM heatmaps generated from features.denseblock4.

PART-WHOLE MODEL

Capsule Network (CapsNet)

Preserves hierarchical part-whole relationships through vector capsules and dynamic routing agreement. Utilizes Conv1-3, 784 Primary Capsules (dim 8), and 13 Disease Capsules (dim 16) with true Grad-CAM++ higher-order gradients.

GRAPH NEURAL NET

Graph Attention Network (GAT)

Converts convolutional feature representations into an image-region graph (49 spatial nodes) with 8-neighbor spatial adjacency. Uses multi-head graph attention to model dependencies across thoracic anatomical sectors.
